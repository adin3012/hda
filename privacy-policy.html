Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Himanshu Deka Academy ("HDA", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Fill out the application form (name, email, WhatsApp number, fitness details)

  • Contact us by email or social media

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and assess your application

Application data is stored securely via Google Forms / Google Sheets and is accessible only to HDA. We retain your data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for.

We use localStorage on your browser solely to remember whether you have already seen our entry popup. No tracking cookies or third-party ad cookies are used.

Our site links to Instagram and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those platforms.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at himanshudeka76@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at himanshudeka76@gmail.com.
